## Task 4: TTS Evaluation

### Objective

This task looks at the voice quality for the assistant. The project needs a voice that sounds natural in Urdu and UrduLish, with good pacing and clear pronunciation.

### Options considered

The main comparison is between Fish Audio and ElevenLabs.

The most important criteria are:

- latency
- naturalness
- emotional tone
- multilingual quality
- Urdu pronunciation
- Urdu-English switching
- price

### Testing approach

Use the same sample lines for both voices and compare the result with the same scoring method.

### Example test sentences

> "Assalam-o-Alaikum sir, aap kis area mein property search kar rahe hain?"

> "Aap ka budget approximately kitna hai?"

> "Ji sir, main aap ke liye available properties check karti hoon."

> "DHA mein ek five marla house available hai, jo aap ke budget ke kaafi close hai."

> "Agar aap chahein to main kal afternoon mein property visit schedule kar sakti hoon."

### What matters most

For a live calling assistant, time-to-first-audio and natural rhythm matter a lot. A voice can be technically capable but still feel awkward in live use if the timing is off.

### Practical takeaway

The voice should feel human enough that callers do not immediately notice the bot. The project is not trying to sound perfect in every scenario. It is aiming for a natural enough performance in the real-estate sales context.
